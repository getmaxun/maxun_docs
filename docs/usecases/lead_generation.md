# Lead Generation

Maxun extracts company and contact information from online directories, social media and business listings to build targeted lead lists for sales and marketing campaigns. Maxun handles the data extraction, so you can focus on outreach.

## What You Can Collect

- Company names, websites and descriptions
- Locations and categories
- Public contact details listed on directory pages
- Profile information from business listings

## How to Set It Up

1. **Extract the list.** Create an [Extract robot](/robot/extract/robot-actions) on a directory or listing page and capture every entry with pagination.
2. **Get the details.** Use [Deep Extraction](/deep-extraction) on Maxun Cloud to open each listing and capture the details from its own page.
3. **Scale it.** [Duplicate the robot](/robot/extract/robot-duplicate) for other categories or locations with the same page structure.
4. **Send leads to your tools.** Sync to [Google Sheets](/integrations/gsheet) or [Airtable](/integrations/airtable), or trigger an [n8n](/integrations/n8n) workflow for enrichment and outreach.

> Only collect publicly available data, follow each website's terms of service and comply with data protection laws such as GDPR.
