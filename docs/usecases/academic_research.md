# Academic Research

Maxun collects data from scientific journals, online databases and public archives for your research projects. Automate data gathering for literature reviews, statistical analysis and trend identification, without writing code.

## What You Can Collect

- Paper titles, authors, abstracts and publication dates
- Citation and journal information
- Datasets and tables published on public websites
- Full text of open-access articles as Markdown

## How to Set It Up

1. **Extract search results.** Create an [Extract robot](/robot/extract/robot-actions) on a journal or database search page and capture every result with pagination.
2. **Parse documents.** Use [Document robots](/robot/document) to extract fields from PDFs or convert them to Markdown.
3. **Build datasets.** Export results as CSV or JSON, or sync them to [Google Sheets](/integrations/gsheet) for analysis.
4. **Stay up to date.** [Schedule](/robot-schedule) runs to collect new publications automatically.
