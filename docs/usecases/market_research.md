# Market Research

Good market research needs fresh data from many sources. Maxun collects product specifications, customer reviews, pricing and competitor activity from e-commerce and review sites, and keeps it up to date automatically.

## What You Can Collect

- Product specifications and feature lists
- Customer reviews and ratings
- Pricing across retailers
- Competitor product launches and catalog changes

## How to Set It Up

1. **Pick the right robot.** Use [Extract](/robot/extract/robot-actions) for structured lists such as products or reviews, [Crawl](/robot/crawl) to collect a whole site or section, and [Search](/robot/search/search-introduction) to discover relevant pages for a topic.
2. **Describe the data in plain English.** [AI Mode](/robot/extract/llm-extraction) builds the robot from a prompt like "Extract product names, prices and ratings".
3. **Keep it fresh.** [Schedule](/robot-schedule) runs and turn on [Monitoring](/monitoring) to track changes over time.
4. **Analyze it.** Export to CSV or JSON, sync to [Google Sheets](/integrations/gsheet), or feed it into an AI pipeline with [LangChain](/integrations/langchain) or [LlamaIndex](/integrations/llamaindex).
