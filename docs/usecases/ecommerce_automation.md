# E-Commerce Price & Stock Monitoring

Prices and stock levels change constantly across online stores. Maxun monitors product pages for you and turns them into structured data you can act on: competitor prices, discounts, availability and new products.

## What You Can Collect

- Product names, prices, discounts and currencies
- Stock and availability status
- Ratings and review counts
- New products added to a category

## How to Set It Up

1. **Extract the product data.** Create an [Extract robot](/robot/extract/robot-actions) on a category or search results page and capture the product list with pagination. Or describe the fields in plain English with [AI Mode](/robot/extract/llm-extraction).
2. **Reuse it across stores and categories.** [Duplicate the robot](/robot/extract/robot-duplicate) for other pages with the same structure.
3. **Schedule it.** Run the robot [daily or hourly](/robot-schedule).
4. **Get alerts on changes.** Turn on [Monitoring](/monitoring) to see exactly what changed since the last run.
5. **Send the data where you need it.** Push results to [Google Sheets](/integrations/gsheet), [Airtable](/integrations/airtable), [n8n](/integrations/n8n) or your own app via [webhooks](/api/webhooks).
