# Real Estate Data Extraction

Maxun extracts property listings, agent contact details, rental prices and market trends from real estate portals. Power your real estate analytics, investment strategies or lead generation with fresh data.

## What You Can Collect

- Listing titles, prices and price per square foot
- Location, size, bedrooms and amenities
- Rental prices and availability
- Agent and broker details

## How to Set It Up

1. **Extract the listings.** Create an [Extract robot](/robot/extract/robot-actions) on a search results page and capture every listing with pagination.
2. **Get full property details.** Use [Deep Extraction](/deep-extraction) on Maxun Cloud to visit each listing page and capture its details.
3. **Cover more locations.** [Duplicate the robot](/robot/extract/robot-duplicate) for other cities or neighborhoods.
4. **Track the market.** [Schedule](/robot-schedule) daily runs and turn on [Monitoring](/monitoring) to spot new listings and price drops.
